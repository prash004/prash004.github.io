DHL IT Services · Pricing analytics

Willingness to Pay

Automated data pipelines for price-sensitivity analysis so pricing teams could see willingness-to-pay signals without a manual refresh ritual.

Challenge

Price-sensitivity inputs lived across warehouses and arrived too slowly for pricing cycles.

Approach

Built and scheduled ingest/transform jobs in Python and Airflow, landing curated tables in Snowflake that downstream models and reports could trust.

Outcome

A repeatable feed for optimized pricing strategies instead of ad-hoc extracts.
