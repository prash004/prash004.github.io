Reweigh Dashboard

An interactive Power BI surface for parcel reweigh analysis, built so operations can see weight exceptions and billing impact without waiting on a static pack.

Challenge

Reweigh exceptions were hard to scan across volume. Planners needed a live view of where parcels drifted from declared weight and what that meant for revenue and network decisions.

Approach

Modeled the reweigh facts in SQL, then layered Power BI visuals for exception rates, lanes, and billing impact. Kept the dashboard operational — filters operations already use, not a gallery of charts.

Outcome

Faster reads on reweigh exceptions and clearer input into operational decisions.
