DHL IT Services · Forecasting

ACS Swing

Forecast void space in aircraft Unit Load Devices so load planners can pack ULDs tighter and waste less air.

Challenge

Unused volume in ULDs is lost capacity. Planners needed a forward view of void space, not only a post-load report.

Approach

Designed forecasting workflows in Python, tracked experiments with MLflow, and productionized the path so the forecast could sit in operational planning.

Outcome

Better load utilization signals for aircraft ULDs.
