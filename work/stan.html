STAN — Shipment Type Analytics

Workflows that classify shipments as B2B or B2C, improving how volume is planned and routed through the network.

Challenge

Shipment type was inconsistent across sources, which made network planning noisier than it needed to be.

Approach

SQL and Python workflows to standardize features and classify B2B vs B2C, then feed the labels into operational planning views.

Outcome

Clearer shipment mix for planning teams.
